TITLE: GenDrive 3-Phase Induction Motor 7.5kW Heavy Duty

This heavy duty motor is built for continuous industrial operation in
foundries and rolling mills. It is a rugged, foot mounted 3-phase
induction motor rated for tough ambient conditions.

Rated Power: 7.5 kW
Rated Voltage: 41500 V
Rated Current: 14.2 A
Supply Frequency: 50 Hz
Rated Speed  1480
IP Rating: IP-65
Insulation Class: F
Net Weight: 68 kg
Standard: IEC 60034

The unit ships with a standard terminal box on the right-hand side and
is compatible with VFD operation up to 50m cable length without an
output filter, though a sine wave filter is recommended beyond that.
